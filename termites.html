Before you decide.

Can a photo confirm termites?

A photo can help explain your concern but may not be enough to identify the pest or the extent of activity.

Will every space be inspected?

Access depends on the building. Ask the provider to record any areas that cannot be inspected.

What should a quotation explain?

Ask for the proposed scope, method, limitations, preparation, follow-up and full cost in writing.

Accessible timber

Read the visible changes

Photograph accessible changes and describe their exact location. Surface appearance alone does not establish the pest or the extent of damage. Ask what further assessment may be needed.
