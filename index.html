Ask. Compare. Understand.

Methods depend on the pest, site and provider assessment.

Target the activity.

Bait systems are designed around the identified pest and its behaviour. Ask a provider about placement, access restrictions and follow-up.

  • Which pest has been identified?
  • How will the system be checked?
  • What access precautions apply?
Discuss your concern

Understand the pattern.

Monitoring helps a provider understand where and when activity occurs, and whether the agreed plan needs to change.

  • Where are the activity points?
  • When should monitoring be reviewed?
  • How will findings be recorded?
Discuss your concern

Close the openings.

Exclusion focuses on the gaps that allow pests to enter. Ask what can be sealed, what needs ventilation and how it will be checked.

  • Which entry points were found?
  • What materials suit the opening?
  • How can new gaps be prevented?
Discuss your concern

How does this platform work?

Explore the guides, choose the service that fits your concern and send an enquiry. We can help you discuss your needs with an independent provider. Availability varies by location.

Do you carry out pest treatments?

No. We are an independent discovery platform. Any inspection, quotation or treatment is agreed directly with the provider you choose.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Tell us what you have noticed, where it is happening, how long it has been present and whether children or pets use the space. Avoid including sensitive personal information.

Can I compare different approaches?

Yes. Ask providers about inspection findings, the proposed method, preparation, follow-up and the full written quotation. The right approach depends on the property and the identified pest.
