About the platform

Pestora is an independent discovery platform. We help you explore pest control options and connect with independent providers. We do not carry out treatments.

Information and availability

The guides provide general context and cannot identify a pest, inspect a property or determine a suitable treatment. Provider availability, methods and pricing vary.

Independent services

Any quotation, inspection, treatment, payment or follow-up is agreed directly with the independent provider. Review their credentials, scope and terms before proceeding.

Using this website

Use the website lawfully. Do not submit misleading requests, harmful content or another person’s confidential information. Enquiries do not create a service contract.

Before agreeing to work

Ask the independent provider for a written description of the scope, exclusions, access requirements and cost. Clarify who is responsible for preparation and how questions or changes will be handled.

Illustrations and photographs

Website photographs and interactive pest scenes illustrate topics. They are not evidence of an inspection at your property, verified customer projects or guaranteed treatment outcomes.

Enquiries and responses

Submitting the form expresses an interest in discussing a concern. It does not confirm availability, establish a price or reserve an appointment. Those details must be agreed with the relevant provider.

Questions about an agreement

For questions about work you have commissioned, refer to the provider and the terms of that agreement. For questions about the website itself, use the email address shown in the footer.
