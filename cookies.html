Local preferences

This website stores your cookie preference in your browser so the notice does not appear on every page. The preference is stored locally and is not sent to a tracking service.

Analytics and advertising

This version does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Fonts, scripts and website images are served with the site.

Changing your choice

You can reset the preference using the button below or clear website data in your browser. Declining optional cookies does not prevent navigation or enquiries.

Questions

For questions about website preferences, contact hello@pestora.example.

What the preference stores

The local entry pestora-consent records accepted or declined. The site reads it to decide whether to display the notice again. It does not hold the name, email or message entered in the enquiry form.

Where it applies

A preference is specific to the browser and website origin where it was saved. Another device, another browser or a private browsing session may show the notice again.

Browser controls

You can remove local website data through your browser settings. Doing so resets the notice on a later visit. Browser settings may also restrict local storage; the site remains navigable when a preference cannot be saved.

Essential site delivery

Your browser requests the pages, images, fonts and scripts needed to display the site. Those requests are distinct from advertising or analytics cookies. The hosting service may process technical connection information to deliver the website.
