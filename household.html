Before you decide.

Is one sighting enough to enquire?

Yes. Describe what you saw and where. A provider can explain whether an inspection is appropriate.

Should I move furniture or empty cupboards?

Wait for the provider’s preparation instructions. Requirements vary by method and the affected space.

How is follow-up decided?

Ask the provider what signs they will review and when. There is no single schedule for every pest or property.

Food storage

Look at the pattern

Loose packaging, crumbs and quiet shelves can provide useful context. Record the cupboard and the time of sightings. Ask how food storage should be prepared before any proposed work.

Moisture & shelter

Follow the room

Tell the provider about recurring activity near plumbing or appliances. Avoid moving heavy equipment yourself. A clear description helps define an appropriate inspection.
