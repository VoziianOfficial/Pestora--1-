Read how this discovery platform works, what an enquiry means and where to find help with your questions.

Pestora NetworkUpdated September 2026

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry, you provide your name, email, service selection, property context and message. Please include only information needed to explain your request.

How information is used

Enquiry information is used to respond to your request and, where explained and agreed, support contact with an independent provider. It is not a booking or a promise of service.

Providers and your choice

Independent providers operate under their own terms and privacy notices. Review those notices before supplying further information or agreeing to work.

Your questions and requests

Contact hello@pestora.example to ask about your information or request a correction or deletion. Applicable rights and retention requirements depend on the operating jurisdiction.

Enquiry details

The form asks for your name and email so a response can be addressed to you. Your service selection, property type and message provide context. Do not include payment details, identity documents or information about another person without their permission.

Sharing context

If a conversation involves an independent provider, clarify which details are needed and how they will be used before sharing further information. Providers are responsible for their own notices and handling practices.

Browser preferences

The cookie-choice notice stores a local preference. This is separate from the information you submit in the enquiry form. You can clear that local preference without sending a deletion request.

Keeping information accurate

If your email or enquiry details were entered incorrectly, contact us with enough context to identify the request. Avoid sending unnecessary documents or confidential material in a correction request.
